Why TradingView's built-in Discord alerts fall short

TradingView alerts can call a webhook, and Discord channels have webhook URLs. Connecting them directly works, with two catches:

  • ✗Text only. Discord gets a line like "AAPL crossing 182". Nobody can see the chart without opening TradingView.
  • ✗You have to write JSON. Discord only accepts messages formatted as {"content": "..."}, so every alert message needs rewriting, and one typo means the alert silently fails.

{{ app_name }} sits in between: TradingView sends the alert to {{ app_name }}, which opens the chart, takes a screenshot, and posts both to Discord.

How to set it up (about 2 minutes)

  1. 1

    Create a Discord webhook

    In Discord: Channel settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy Webhook URL. Paste it into your {{ app_name }} dashboard.

  2. 2

    Send a test

    Click "Send test alert" and a chart appears in your channel within seconds.

  3. 3

    Add the webhook to your TradingView alerts

    Create an alert (Alt + A). In Notifications, tick Webhook URL and paste your {{ app_name }} URL. Keep your usual message, or paste the one-line message from your dashboard.
